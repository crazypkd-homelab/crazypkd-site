This is the first post on the crazypkd homelab blog.

## What This Space Is About

This homelab exists to explore modern systems — cloud infrastructure, automation, and AI-driven tooling — through curiosity and real-world experimentation. The focus is on understanding how systems work at every layer, how ideas connect, and how complexity emerges through composition.

## What to Expect

Posts here will cover:

- **Cloud Systems** — Infrastructure, networking, deployment pipelines, and reliability engineering.
- **Automation** — Tooling, workflows, CI/CD, and reducing operational friction.
- **AI Systems** — Agents, integrations, and applied AI tooling.
- **Experiments** — Labs, prototypes, and exploratory builds.

## Looking Ahead

This is only the beginning. The structure, content, and direction of this space will continue to evolve as I learn and build more.
